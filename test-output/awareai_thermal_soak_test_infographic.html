Summary

HW team Brno • report 2026-10-12

1. Objective

Compare three Magic box enclosure cooling variants under sustained high ambient temperature and full analytics load, and select one for field deployment.

2. Setup

Climate chamber at 55 °C ambient for 72 h, 6 camera streams. Throttle threshold of the SoC is 90 °C.

3. Results

  • A — passive heatsink: max 97 °C, throttling from hour 9, average inference dropped 25 → 17 FPS.
  • B — heatsink + 40 mm fan: max 81 °C, no throttling, stable 25 FPS; fan noise 32 dBA; BOM +€9 vs A.
  • C — heatsink + heat pipe to enclosure wall: max 84 °C, no throttling, stable 25 FPS; no moving parts; BOM +€14 vs A.

4. Recommendation

Variant C for field deployment. B runs 3 °C cooler, but relies on a fan, and fan MTBF is a concern in roadside cabinets. C meets the thermal requirement without moving parts for +€5 over B.
